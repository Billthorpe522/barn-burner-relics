Lifetime Authenticity Guarantee

Every artifact sold by Barn Burner Relics is guaranteed authentic for life.

If a piece is ever proven not to be authentic, we refund the full purchase price — no time limit. That's the deal, full stop.

The Auction

When does the auction run?

Every Monday at 5 PM Central, Nick posts 20–40 fresh lots to the Barn Burner Relics Facebook group. Bidding stays open until Nick calls each lot.

From fall or winter forward, Nick adds Saturday auctions alongside the Monday schedule. Until then it's Mondays only.

Where is the auction?

The auction lives in the Barn Burner Relics Inc. Facebook group. Joining the group is the only way to bid — every lot is posted there with photos and bid tracking in the comments.

Friend both Nick Gatses and Camden Baker on Facebook before the sale — Camden sends your invoice and you'll want the message to land quickly.

Is there a buyer premium?

No. Barn Burner Relics charges flat shipping only — no buyer premium, no hidden fees, no surcharge on top of your bid. Your winning bid is what you pay for the piece.

This is the major difference between us and auction houses like eBay or LiveAuctioneers, which can take 20%+ in premiums and fees combined.

Bidding

How do I place a bid?

Comment on the lot photo in the Facebook group. That's it — your bid is whatever number you type in the comment. There's no app, no registration, no pre-approval.

Is there a timer on each lot?

No timer. Bidding stays open until Nick calls the lot. This is the opposite of eBay's snipe-everything-in-the-last-3-seconds style — the auction is a conversation, not a countdown.

How do I claim a Buy-It-Now piece?

Type SOLD in the comments on the lot, or write that you want the item. If you're the first to do it, the piece is yours.

What if I change my mind after bidding?

Bid retraction is at Nick's discretion. Bids are commitments — don't drop a number you can't stand behind. If you made a genuine mistake, reach out to him right away in the group.

Shipping

How much does shipping cost?

$10 flat rate anywhere in the United States, via UPS or USPS. One box, any box — single points, bannerstones, axes, birdstones, and small collections all ship at $10 flat.

Pottery ships at $15 flat because it takes extra packing and a double-boxed shipper to get it there whole. Both rates cover any box, regardless of weight.

If you win multiple pieces, we'll combine them into one shipment when the box size makes sense.

How quickly do orders ship?

Orders ship weekly. Once Camden sends your invoice and payment clears, your piece is boxed and on its way within a few business days.

Do you ship internationally?

The $10 flat rate covers U.S. shipping. For international orders, message Nick before bidding to confirm shipping cost to your country.

Local Pickup

Can I pick up my piece in person?

Yes. Local pickup is available in the Manhattan, IL area. After the sale ends, Camden (or Nick) will message you on Facebook to arrange a pickup time.

What Kinds of Pieces

What do you sell?

Authentic Native American artifacts, personally vetted by Nick Gatses. Categories typical of a Monday auction include:

Arrowheads · spear points · bannerstones · axes · birdpoints · birdstones · beads · pottery.

Each piece is photographed from multiple angles with provenance noted where available (previous collections, find sites, published references).

How do you decide what's authentic?

Nick has been collecting and vetting for decades. Every piece is examined in person — material, flaking pattern, wear, provenance. If it doesn't pass his eye, it doesn't go in the sale. The lifetime authenticity guarantee stands behind every piece that does.

Invoices & Payment

Who sends my invoice?

Camden Baker sends invoices after each Monday sale. He'll message you on Facebook with the invoice total (winning bid + $10 flat shipping) and payment instructions.

This is why it's worth friending Camden on Facebook ahead of time — if he can't reach you, your win is delayed.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payment details are listed on each invoice from Camden. The standard methods are Venmo, PayPal, and Zelle. Cash works for local pickup.

How long do I have to pay?

Pay the invoice promptly once it arrives. Unpaid lots may be offered to the next bidder — auction wins are not indefinite holds.

Other Questions

Can I email Nick instead of Facebook?

Yes — for buying and selling inquiries, email bbryoutube@gmail.com. The Facebook group is the auction surface; email is better for private sales, single-piece inquiries, and collection-level conversations.

Can I see the pieces in person before bidding?

Yes — Nick exhibits at the annual Collinsville Prehistoric Artifact Show (March) and other regional shows throughout the year. The full show schedule is on the Shows page. Coming to a show is the best way to handle pieces, ask Nick questions in person, and see what's coming through the vault.

What if I have a question that isn't here?

Post it in the Barn Burner Relics Facebook group, or email bbryoutube@gmail.com. Nick answers both directly.
